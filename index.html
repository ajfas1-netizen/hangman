Hangdle

Body 0/6
Rope 0/5

How to play

Guess a letter and the slot it sits in.

  • Hit The letter is in that slot. It locks in. Costs nothing.
  • Near The letter is in the word, but not there. The rope tightens.
  • Miss The letter isn't in the word. A body part goes up.

Two ways to hang: 6 body parts or 5 rope notches. They fill independently, so a run of near misses will kill you just as surely as a run of wrong letters. The rope is the shorter track on purpose — near misses come up less often than outright wrong letters, so it takes fewer of them to matter.

The catch

Landing a letter tells you nothing about how many more of it there are. Hit the O in slot 3 of SPOON and the second O is still out there, unmarked. Nothing ever tells you a count.

One consequence worth knowing: once every copy of a letter is on the board, guessing it again is a miss, not a near. There's nothing left to find, and the game won't pretend otherwise.

Calling the word

Think you've got it? Call the whole word. Right wins on the spot. Wrong costs 2 body parts.

Modes

The daily is always Normal. New game lets you pick a length and a mode: Hard shortens the rope to four, so near misses bite sooner; Zen removes both limits entirely — nothing can kill you, so you can just work the word out.

Reading the board

The keyboard tracks what you know about each letter overall. Each slot tracks what you've ruled out for that slot — select a slot to see its eliminations.

A new word every day. Length ramps through the week: five letters early, seven by the weekend.
